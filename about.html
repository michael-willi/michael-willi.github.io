About Me

Builder, student, and aspiring investor. Bloomington, Indiana and Austin, Texas.

What I'm working on

I was selected for the IU Innovates Incubator, where I am currently doing market research and product discovery for the new ventures I am working on. The method is the one that worked last time: talk to operators before building anything, and let positioning, not features, decide what gets built.

Impact Margin

In 2026 I founded and led Impact Margin, software that read a business's operating and utility bills, flagged overbilling, cost spikes, and late fees, and showed the owner exactly where the savings were and how to capture them. In a few months my cofounder and CTO and I took it from an idea we were testing on campus to a live web app with a paying customer, a local coffee shop that came on as a design partner and shaped the roadmap with us. I owned product, go-to-market, and pricing, and we ran on a simple subscription at $5 per month per location with a 30-day free trial.

Before building anything we ran direct customer discovery, including dozens of operator conversations and live testing on campus, and the biggest lesson was that positioning, not features, drove demand. It did not last as a business, but it shipped and it earned a paying customer. Running it taught me more than planning it ever did.

Background

I have been building since high school: Ethereum mining rigs, products sourced from overseas manufacturers, small ecommerce brands. Some failed fast. Some made real money. All of them showed me a business from the inside, and that view is why I am drawn to investing. Operating teaches you the gap between how a deal is presented and how it actually performs. That gap is where I want to work.

The specifics: I built and ran an Ethereum mining rig, sourced products from overseas manufacturers through Alibaba to launch direct-to-consumer apparel and sporting goods brands, and used buying bots to secure hard-to-find RTX 3080 graphics cards for resale during the 2020 to 2021 shortage. I was launching consumer products before I could drive.

Through high school I spent my summers, and part-time during the school year, as a legal assistant at Willi Law Firm, my parents' estate planning and probate practice in Austin, guiding clients one-on-one through estate planning and regulated federal filings and serving as an authorized witness on more than 100 estate plans. In Summer 2026 I went back as a builder-operator and shipped a privacy-first desktop app, local AI and zero cloud, that turns thousands of pages of medical records into a patient chronology for probate litigation. It is in use on live matters. I also owned the hiring funnel for the firm's legal assistant role, and the firm hired from my top-10 brief.

Research and writing

The same lens runs through my research and writing. I published a venture thesis on the startups pulling lithium out of Permian Basin oilfield wastewater. It drew inbound from senior oil and gas executives, led to calls with executives and a startup operator in the play, and to a shadow day on Rio Energy International's oil trading desk in Houston. The second edition, published in July 2026, folds in the technical feedback industry readers sent.

I have also underwritten a twelve-unit multifamily acquisition near campus from rent roll to returns, modeling its in-place performance against a value-add scenario with a full pro forma. Both are on my LinkedIn. On the finance side, I completed Wall Street Prep's Excel course and am working through the Accounting Crash Course, with the Private Equity Masterclass next.
