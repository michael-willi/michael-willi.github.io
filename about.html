About Me

Founder, builder, and student in Austin, Texas.

What I'm building

I'm the founder and CEO of Impact Margin, software that helps independent businesses find the money quietly leaking out of their bills. The product reads a business's operating and utility bills, flags overbilling, cost spikes, and late fees, and shows the owner exactly where the savings are and how to capture them. We took it from an idea tested on campus to a live web app with a first paying customer, a local coffee shop that now acts as a design partner shaping the roadmap. It is priced simply: $15 per month per location, with a 30-day free trial.

Impact Margin is a Delaware C-Corp. I own product, go-to-market, and pricing, and I build alongside my cofounder and CTO. We are also prototyping a paperless receipt system that plugs into the point of sale where a thermal printer would normally connect, cutting paper and waste while bringing customers back through coupons and offers. Cutting waste is good for the planet. It is also good for the bottom line.

Background

I have been starting businesses since high school. Some failed fast, some made real money, and all of them taught me something. I built and ran an Ethereum mining rig, sourced products from overseas manufacturers through Alibaba to launch direct-to-consumer apparel and sporting goods brands, and used buying bots to secure hard-to-find RTX 3080 graphics cards for resale during the 2020 to 2021 shortage. I was launching consumer products before I could drive.

Before Impact Margin, I spent nearly five years as a Legal Assistant at Willi Law Firm in Austin. I guided clients one-on-one through estate planning and regulated federal filings, walked them through fast-changing rules so they understood what they were signing, and served as an authorized witness on more than 100 estate plans.

Education

I'm a sophomore at Indiana University Bloomington, studying Merchandising and Product Development at the Eskenazi School of Art, Architecture and Design with a 4.0 GPA. I'm pursuing a Business Minor and a Real Estate Certificate through the Kelley School of Business, and I was named to the Executive Dean's List in Spring 2026. I'm part of the IU Innovates Entrepreneurship Bootcamp and the Retail Studies Organization.

I also attended the National Student Leadership Conference for Business and Entrepreneurship at Columbia University, where my team reached the pitch competition finals.
